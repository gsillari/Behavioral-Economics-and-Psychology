# Peter M. Todd

## Who or what it is

[[Peter M. Todd]] is a cognitive scientist at Indiana University whose research explores adaptive behavior, simple heuristics, and decision making in structured environments.
He has worked across cognitive science, biology, artificial life, and behavioral modeling.
He is one of the main collaborators who helped turn ecological rationality into a concrete research program rather than a philosophical slogan.

## Why it matters here

Todd matters here because he helps articulate the environment side of the ecological program clearly and accessibly. His collaboration with [[Gerd Gigerenzer]] is especially useful for the current vault because it sharpens the relation between simple heuristics, bounded minds, and structured environments without requiring a large new subtree.
