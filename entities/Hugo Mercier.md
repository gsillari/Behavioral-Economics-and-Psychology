# Hugo Mercier

## Who or what it is

[[Hugo Mercier]] is a French cognitive scientist and CNRS research director at the Institut Jean Nicod in Paris.
His work focuses on reasoning, communication, epistemic vigilance, and social cognition, most famously in collaboration with [[Dan Sperber]].
He helped turn the argumentative view of reasoning into a serious contender in contemporary rationality debates.

## Why it matters here

Mercier matters here because he shifts the rationality debate from standards and errors to function. His work asks why human reasoning exists at all and why it performs so differently in argumentative and nonargumentative settings.

## Key relationships

The closest links are to [[Argumentative theory of reasoning]], [[Rationality wars]], and [[Behavioral Economics]]. His work also belongs near [[Fast and slow thinking]], [[Motivated reasoning]], and [[Confirmation bias]] because it helps explain why reflective thought is not always an unbiased corrective.
