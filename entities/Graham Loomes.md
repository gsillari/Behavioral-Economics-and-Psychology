# Graham Loomes

## Who or what it is

[[Graham Loomes]] is a British economist at the University of Warwick specializing in behavioral economics, risky choice, and policy valuation.
He is best known for co-developing [[Regret theory]] and for later work on health, safety, and environmental preferences under uncertainty.
He shows one route away from standard expected utility that does not simply collapse into prospect theory.

## Why it matters here

Loomes matters here because he represents a more radical alternative to expected utility than independence-relaxing generalizations: the evaluative unit itself shifts to regret and rejoicing across chosen and forgone outcomes.

## Key relationships

The closest links are to [[Regret theory]], [[Expected utility theory]], and [[Allais paradox]].
