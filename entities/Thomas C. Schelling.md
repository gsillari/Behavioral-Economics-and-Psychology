# Thomas C. Schelling

Thomas C. Schelling was an economist and strategist whose work is central to the analysis of bargaining, deterrence, coordination, commitment, and strategic interaction. In the current vault, his first major role is as the author of [[Schelling 1960]], especially Chapter 4 on interdependent decision and [[Coordination games]]. [[Lewis 1969]] is now the immediate successor source, turning Schelling's focal-point analysis into a philosophical theory of [[Convention]].

For PPE students, Schelling matters because he shows how rational strategy depends on communication, salience, focal points, credibility, and mutual expectations. That places him between formal [[Game theory]], political strategy, social coordination, and institutional analysis.
