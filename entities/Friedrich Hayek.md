# Friedrich Hayek

## Who or what it is

[[Friedrich Hayek]] is the economist and social theorist whose term "spontaneous order" frames [[Sugden 1989]].
In the current vault, Hayek matters as the figure who pushes against the idea that social institutions can always be rationally appraised or designed from an external standpoint.
