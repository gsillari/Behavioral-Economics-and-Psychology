# David Hume

## Who or what it is

[[David Hume]] is the eighteenth-century philosopher whose account of justice, property, and convention is a central background source for [[Sugden 1989]].
In the current vault, Hume matters because he gives the convention branch its clearest older political-philosophy ancestor: rules of property and justice can arise as conventions and later acquire moral force.
