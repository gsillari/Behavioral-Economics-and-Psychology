# Peter C. Wason

[[Peter C. Wason]] is the psychologist whose conditional-reasoning experiments gave the vault the [[Wason selection task]]. In the current architecture, he matters because his work provides an early experimental bridge between formal logic, the psychology of reasoning, and later debates over human rationality.

The key source is [[Wason 1968]]. Its role is not merely historical. It gives [[Confirmation bias]] a classic rule-testing paradigm and creates one of the main experimental objects later reinterpreted by [[Cosmides and Tooby 2000]], dual-process theory, and the wider [[Rationality wars]].
