# Leaf Van Boven

## Who or what it is

[[Leaf Van Boven]] is a social psychologist at the University of Colorado Boulder whose research examines emotion, judgment, social inference, and decision making.
His work on empathy gaps and buyer-seller perspective taking helps connect valuation differences to social cognition rather than ownership alone.

## Why it matters here

Van Boven matters here because [[Van Boven et al. 2000]] adds a social-cognitive explanation for why owners and buyers may remain apart in valuation.

## Key relationships

The tightest links are to [[Endowment effect]], [[Endowment effect]], and [[WTA-WTP asymmetry]].
