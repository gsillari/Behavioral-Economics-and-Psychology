# Adam Smith

## Who or what it is

[[Adam Smith]] is the eighteenth-century moral philosopher and political economist whose work matters in the current vault mainly through [[Ashraf et al. 2005]].
That paper uses *The Theory of Moral Sentiments* to complicate the textbook image of Smith as a theorist of narrow self-interest.
In this vault, Smith should function as a historical bridge between moral psychology and behavioral economics.

## Why it matters here

Smith matters because his account of the passions, the impartial spectator, sympathy, justice, self-command, status-seeking, and adaptation gives the vault an earlier precedent for themes that later behavioral economics formalizes or tests experimentally.
The point is not that Smith already had prospect theory, dual-process theory, or social-preference models.
The point is that economic agency in Smith is psychologically and socially thick before the later [[Paretian turn]], [[Ordinal utility theory]], [[Revealed preference theory]], and axiomatic decision theory narrow the official language of economic modeling.
