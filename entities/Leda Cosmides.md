# Leda Cosmides

[[Leda Cosmides]] is a central figure in evolutionary psychology and the adaptationist analysis of reasoning. In the current vault, her main role is through [[Cosmides and Tooby 2000]], which uses social exchange and the [[Wason selection task]] to argue that human reasoning includes domain-specific machinery for cheater detection.

Cosmides matters here because she helps shift the interpretation of reasoning experiments. Poor performance on abstract formal tasks need not show global irrationality if performance depends on whether the task fits an evolved problem structure. That makes her work important for [[Evolutionary psychology]], [[Rationality wars]], and the relationship between domain-specific reasoning and [[Fast and slow thinking]].
