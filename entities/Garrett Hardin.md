# Garrett Hardin

## Who or what it is

[[Garrett Hardin]] was an American biologist and ecologist best known in this wiki for [[Hardin 1968]], the classic paper that popularized the idea of the [[Tragedy of the commons]].

Hardin matters here less as a behavioral economist than as a source for the institutional logic behind cooperation failures. His commons argument shows that individual rationality can be destructive when a resource is open-access and costs are dispersed across users. That makes him relevant to [[Free riding]], [[Cooperation]], public-goods reasoning, environmental policy, and the broader question of how institutions reshape incentives.

## Interpretive caution

Hardin's population-control argument is historically important but normatively controversial. In this vault, his durable role should be the commons mechanism and the critique of purely technical fixes, not an uncritical endorsement of his reproductive-policy conclusions.
