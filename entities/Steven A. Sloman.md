# Steven A. Sloman

## Who or what it is

[[Steven A. Sloman]] is an American cognitive psychologist and professor at Brown University.
He is best known for work on causal reasoning, conceptual structure, and dual-process models that distinguish associative from rule-based thought.
His 1996 synthesis gave the dual-process side of the rationality debate one of its clearest early cognitive formulations.

## Why it matters here

Sloman matters here because he gives the vault a strong early theoretical case for two systems of reasoning, framed as associative versus rule-based computation. That makes him important for the cognitive-architecture side of the rationality wars.
