# Wim De Neys

## Who or what it is

[[Wim De Neys]] is a Belgian cognitive scientist and CNRS researcher known for work on reasoning, conflict detection, and intuitive logic.
His experiments challenged the crude view that biased answers always reflect simple blindness to normatively relevant structure.
He is influential because he refined dual-process theory from inside the reasoning literature rather than simply defending or rejecting it wholesale.

## Why it matters here

De Neys matters here because he complicates one of the most familiar stories in behavioral economics and cognitive psychology: the idea that intuition is simply heuristic and reflection is simply corrective. His work helps the vault represent a more nuanced picture of norm sensitivity, conflict awareness, and reasoning architecture. His response to Mercier and Sperber also gives the vault a concrete example of how empirical conflict-detection work pushes back against overly social or overly cynical pictures of reasoning.
