# David Dunning

## Who or what it is

[[David Dunning]] is an American social psychologist at the University of Michigan best known for research on miscalibration, self-knowledge, and the Dunning-Kruger effect.
His work studies how people evaluate their own competence, how they infer social reality, and why error can be invisible from the inside.
He deepened the belief side of behavioral research by linking judgment error to metacognitive limits.
