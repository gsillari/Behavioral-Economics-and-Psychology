# Thomas Gilovich

## Who or what it is

[[Thomas Gilovich]] is an American psychologist and longtime professor at Cornell University whose work helped popularize research on everyday judgment errors.
He has written influentially on statistical misperception, bias, social judgment, and the psychology of ordinary belief.
His research extended classic heuristics findings into a broader account of how people interpret evidence in everyday life.

## Why it matters here

Gilovich matters here because the Epley-Gilovich papers make the process side of judgment more precise. Rather than treating all anchoring effects as instances of one generic bias, the papers distinguish kinds of anchors and kinds of cognitive process, and the 2016 paper shows how ordinary reasoning can also be directionally steered without becoming obviously irrational.
