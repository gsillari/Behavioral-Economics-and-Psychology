# Ellen J. Langer

## Who or what it is

[[Ellen J. Langer]] is an American psychologist at Harvard University and was the first woman to receive tenure in Harvard's psychology department.
Her work spans mindfulness, health, aging, judgment, and the classic study of the [[Illusion of control]].
She is important because she showed how people often overread contingency, agency, and control into uncertain situations.

## Why it matters here

Langer matters here because she gives the vault a foundational account of how people come to experience chance as if it were partly under their control. That contribution strengthens the belief side of behavioral economics and connects agency, luck, and judgment under uncertainty.

## Key relationships

The tightest links are to [[Illusion of control]], [[Overconfidence]], and [[Nonstandard beliefs]].
