# Howard Kunreuther

## Who or what it is

[[Howard Kunreuther]] (1938-2023) was an American economist and risk analyst who spent most of his career at the Wharton School.
He became a leading authority on disaster insurance, climate risk, and the behavioral obstacles that prevent sensible preparation for rare but catastrophic losses.
His work brought behavioral ideas about risk directly into public policy, insurance, and institutional design.

## Why it matters here

Kunreuther matters here because he gives the policy branch a concrete catastrophic-risk application where behavioral failures of preparation have direct institutional consequences.
