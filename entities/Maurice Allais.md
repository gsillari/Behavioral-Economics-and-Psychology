# Maurice Allais

## Who or what it is

[[Maurice Allais]] (1911-2010) was a French economist who won the Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences in 1988 for work on markets and the efficient use of resources.
Although he was a major general theorist of economics, he is best known in decision theory for the [[Allais paradox]], his challenge to the descriptive plausibility of expected-utility theory.
That paradox became one of the clearest early demonstrations that formally respectable axioms could conflict with intuitively attractive choices.
Allais spent much of his career at the Ecole des Mines de Paris and remained an unusually independent thinker in relation to the Anglophone mainstream.
He is an important precursor because he put pressure on standard rational-choice theory from within economics before behavioral economics existed as a named field.
