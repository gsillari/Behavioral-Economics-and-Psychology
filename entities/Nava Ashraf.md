# Nava Ashraf

## Who or what it is

[[Nava Ashraf]] is an economist whose current role in the vault is as coauthor of [[Ashraf et al. 2005]], the source note on Adam Smith as a behavioral economist.
In this vault, the paper matters as a historical and conceptual bridge between Smith's moral psychology and later behavioral economics.
