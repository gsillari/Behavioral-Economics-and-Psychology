# Timur Kuran

## Who or what it is

[[Timur Kuran]] is a Turkish-American economist and political scientist at Duke University.
He is best known for work on preference falsification, social change, and the economic and political history of Islamic institutions.
His collaboration with [[Cass R. Sunstein]] on [[Availability cascades]] links behavioral dynamics to public opinion and policy escalation.

## Why it matters here

Kuran matters here because he helps connect individual-level salience effects to collective public dynamics, especially the way social pressures and informational feedback can amplify risk perception into regulatory action.

## Key relationships

The tightest links are to [[Availability cascades]], [[Behavioral public policy]], and [[Availability heuristic]].
