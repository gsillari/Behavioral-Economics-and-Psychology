# Sarah Lichtenstein

## Who or what it is

[[Sarah Lichtenstein]] (1933-2017) was an American psychologist and one of the central founders of behavioral decision research.
She co-founded Decision Research with [[Paul Slovic]] and [[Baruch Fischhoff]] and helped build the institutional home for some of the field's most influential work.
Lichtenstein made lasting contributions to research on preference reversals, constructed preferences, probability judgments, and perceived risk.
She also co-edited major collections that helped define the judgment-and-decision tradition for later generations.
Her work was crucial in showing that valuation and choice often depend on the procedure by which they are elicited.

## Why it matters here

Lichtenstein matters here because her work helps give both the valuation branch and the risk-perception branch deeper architecture than a list of anomalies. In the current wiki, she is central to the move from isolated preference reversals toward the broader claim that preferences can be shaped by elicitation procedure and context, and she also helps connect availability-based judgment to socially important risk domains.
