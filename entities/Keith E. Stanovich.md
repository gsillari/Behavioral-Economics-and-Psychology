# Keith E. Stanovich

## Who or what it is

[[Keith E. Stanovich]] (born 1950) is a cognitive psychologist best known for work on rational thought, individual differences in reasoning, and dual-process theory.
He is Emeritus Professor of Applied Psychology and Human Development at the University of Toronto and former Canada Research Chair of Applied Cognitive Science.
Stanovich first became widely known for influential research on reading and literacy, but later turned to the cognitive foundations of rationality.
His work on [[Myside bias]], dysrationalia, and the measurement of reflective thinking made the rationality debate more psychologically fine-grained.
He is also one of the major figures who tried to distinguish intelligence from rational thinking rather than treating them as the same capacity.
His research helped make rationality into something that could be studied empirically rather than discussed only as a philosophical ideal.
