# Richard F. West

## Who or what it is

[[Richard F. West]] is an American psychologist and professor emeritus at James Madison University.
Much of his best-known work was done with [[Keith E. Stanovich]] on reasoning, rational thought, and individual differences in bias and reflection.
He helped make rationality research more empirical, measurable, and psychologically differentiated.

## Why it matters here

West matters here because his work helps make the rationality branch more empirically disciplined. Instead of treating rationality as a purely abstract philosophical label, the Stanovich-West program asks how stable differences in reasoning performance and bias should bear on the debate.

## Key relationships

The closest links are to [[Rationality wars]], [[Myside bias]], and [[Behavioral Economics]]. Through the Stanovich-West branch, his work also contributes to how the vault understands reflective cognition, belief distortion, and the limits of equating intelligence with rationality.
