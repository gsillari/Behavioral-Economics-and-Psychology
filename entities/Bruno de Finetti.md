# Bruno de Finetti

## Who or what it is

[[Bruno de Finetti]] (1906-1985) was an Italian mathematician, statistician, and actuary best known for the subjective interpretation of probability. He argued that probability should be understood as degree of belief rather than as an objective frequency residing in the world. That move made him one of the foundational figures for modern Bayesian subjectivism and for later discussions of coherence in belief. He also made lasting technical contributions through work on exchangeability and the representation of uncertain belief. His ideas remain central wherever economists and psychologists argue about how uncertain belief should be modeled.

## Why it matters here

de Finetti matters here because he gives the vault a deeper benchmark for rational belief under uncertainty. Behavioral economics often appears as a challenge to classical rationality, but that rationality benchmark is not only about choice axioms and utility. It also includes views about what coherent belief should look like, and de Finetti is central to that side of the story.
