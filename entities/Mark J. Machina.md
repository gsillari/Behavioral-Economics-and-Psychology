# Mark J. Machina

## Who or what it is

[[Mark J. Machina]] is an American economist known for major work in non-expected-utility decision theory.
At the University of California, San Diego, he became one of the leading theorists exploring how utility analysis might survive without the classical independence axiom.
He represents the most elegant formal attempt to repair expected-utility reasoning rather than replace it entirely.

## Why it matters here

Machina matters here because he represents a conservative but important response to anomaly: preserve as much of utility analysis as possible while weakening the axiom most directly challenged by the [[Allais paradox]].
