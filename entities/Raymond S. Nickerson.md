# Raymond S. Nickerson

## Who or what it is

[[Raymond S. Nickerson]] was an American psychologist and author who worked in human factors, cognition, and applied psychology, including roles at BBN and Tufts University.
He is especially well known here for his influential review of [[Confirmation bias]] and for writing that made reasoning research unusually accessible.
His work clarified one of the most persistent and wide-ranging families of belief distortion in modern psychology.

## Why it matters here

Nickerson matters here because he gives the belief branch a durable synthesis source on selective evidence processing rather than only scattered experiments or adjacent theoretical papers.
