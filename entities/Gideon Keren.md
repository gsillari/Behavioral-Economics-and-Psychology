# Gideon Keren

## Who or what it is

[[Gideon Keren]] is an Israeli-Dutch psychologist and decision researcher associated with Tilburg University.
His work spans judgment under uncertainty, risk, and decision theory, but he is especially visible here as a critic of simple two-system thinking.
He helped articulate skeptical, conceptually careful objections to loose dual-process explanations.

## Why it matters here

Keren matters here because he strengthens the internal critique of fast-and-slow theorizing. His work pushes the vault to treat dual-process language as something to scrutinize, not just to inherit.
