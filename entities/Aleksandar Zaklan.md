# Aleksandar Zaklan

## Who or what it is

[[Aleksandar Zaklan]] is an economist at the University of Rostock whose research focuses on environmental economics, market design, and the effects of policy instruments aimed at reducing externalities.
His work brings contemporary empirical evidence to questions about entitlements, trading, and [[Coase theorem]] in environmental markets.

## Why it matters here

Zaklan matters here because his work gives the vault a contemporary institutional test of whether initial entitlements matter in a real cap-and-trade market.

## Key relationships

The tightest links are to [[Coase theorem]], [[Coase theorem]], and [[Transaction costs]].
