# John von Neumann

## Who or what it is

[[John von Neumann]] was a mathematician whose collaboration with [[Oskar Morgenstern]] produced [[von Neumann and Morgenstern 1944]], one of the founding works of game theory and the canonical source for [[Von Neumann-Morgenstern utility theory]].

## Why it matters here

Von Neumann matters in the vault because he helps anchor the formal benchmark side of behavioral economics. The VNM utility appendix gives the risk-side expected-utility branch its canonical primary source, while the broader book shifts rationality from isolated choice to strategic interdependence.

## Related pages

The main source is [[von Neumann and Morgenstern 1944]]. The closest concept pages are [[Von Neumann-Morgenstern utility theory]], [[Expected utility theory]], and [[Utility theory]]. The main coauthor page is [[Oskar Morgenstern]].
