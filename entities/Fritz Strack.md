# Fritz Strack

## Who or what it is

[[Fritz Strack]] is a German social psychologist and professor emeritus at the University of Wurzburg.
His research spans social cognition, judgment, affect, and the interplay between reflective and impulsive determinants of behavior.
In this literature he is especially important for work with [[Thomas Mussweiler]] on anchoring and selective accessibility.

## Why it matters here

Strack matters here because [[Strack and Mussweiler 1997]] gives the anchoring branch a strong selective-accessibility model rather than leaving it organized only around later adjustment-based accounts.
