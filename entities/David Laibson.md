# David Laibson

## Who or what it is

[[David Laibson]] is an American economist whose work has been especially important in behavioral economics, intertemporal choice, savings behavior, and behavioral public finance.
He is one of the economists most closely associated with present bias and with bringing behavioral ideas into mainstream economic modeling and teaching.
In the current vault, he matters less as a source of many separate leaf concepts than as a figure who helps connect classroom-facing behavioral economics to formal work on self-control and dynamic choice.

## Why it matters here

Laibson matters here because [[Laibson and List 2015]] gives the vault a compact six-principle teaching frame for behavioral economics, and because his wider work helps anchor the self-control and intertemporal-choice branch.
