# Oskar Morgenstern

## Who or what it is

[[Oskar Morgenstern]] was an economist whose collaboration with [[John von Neumann]] produced [[von Neumann and Morgenstern 1944]], a foundational work for game theory, strategic rationality, and expected-utility theory under risk.

## Why it matters here

Morgenstern matters because the book is not only a mathematical contribution. It is also an attempt to reconstruct economic theory around strategic interaction, conflict, coalition, and rational behavior under uncertainty. In the current vault, his most immediate role is as coauthor of the source that anchors [[Von Neumann-Morgenstern utility theory]].
