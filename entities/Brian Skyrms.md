# Brian Skyrms

## Who or what it is

Brian Skyrms is a philosopher of science and decision theorist whose work is central to evolutionary game theory, signaling, social contract, and the emergence of conventions.

## Why it matters here

In the current vault, Skyrms matters because [[Skyrms 2014]] extends the Schelling-Lewis branch from focal points and convention into evolutionary dynamics. His analysis asks how fairness, cooperation, ownership, and convention can emerge through replication, learning, correlation, and cultural transmission rather than through explicit agreement alone.

## Key relationships

Skyrms is a bridge between [[Game theory]], [[Evolutionary game theory]], [[Coordination games]], [[Convention]], [[Cooperation]], and [[Fairness]]. He also connects the institutional branch of PPE to the behavioral-game evidence on the [[Ultimatum game]] and to ownership-sensitive valuation in the [[Endowment effect]] literature.
