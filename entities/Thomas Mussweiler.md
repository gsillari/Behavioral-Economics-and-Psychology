# Thomas Mussweiler

## Who or what it is

[[Thomas Mussweiler]] is a German social psychologist known for research on social comparison, judgment, and anchoring.
He has held senior academic positions in Cologne and London, and his work often investigates how comparative standards shape evaluation.
His studies with [[Fritz Strack]] provided one of the most influential mechanistic accounts of the anchoring effect.

## Why it matters here

Mussweiler matters here because [[Strack and Mussweiler 1997]] helps explain anchoring through selective accessibility rather than only through insufficient adjustment.
