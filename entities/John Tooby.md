# John Tooby

[[John Tooby]] is a central figure in evolutionary psychology and the adaptationist account of cognitive architecture. In the current vault, he enters through [[Cosmides and Tooby 2000]], which argues that social reasoning should be studied through theories of adaptive function and domain-specific computational design.

Tooby matters here because the social-contract interpretation of the [[Wason selection task]] gives the vault a clear example of how evolutionary psychology challenges content-free accounts of rationality. The point is not simply that people reason better with familiar content, but that performance may improve when a task activates machinery specialized for a recurrent adaptive problem such as detecting cheaters in reciprocal exchange.
