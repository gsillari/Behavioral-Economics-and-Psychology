# Ward Edwards

## Who or what it is

[[Ward Edwards]] was an American psychologist and decision researcher who helped found modern behavioral decision theory.
He is well known for early work on Bayesian updating, conservatism in information processing, and the contrast between normative and descriptive models of judgment.
He belongs to the earlier generation of decision research that set the stage for the later heuristics-and-biases tradition.

## Why it matters here

Edwards matters here because he gives the vault a classic benchmark-oriented comparison between human belief revision and Bayesian updating. That makes him important on the formal-belief side of the behavioral economics story.
