# Yaacov Schul

## Who or what it is

[[Yaacov Schul]] is an Israeli social psychologist and professor emeritus at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem whose research centers on judgment, person perception, trust, and the use of invalid information.
He is best known here as a careful critic of oversimplified dual-process claims and as a contributor to the anchoring and reasoning literature.

## Why it matters here

Schul matters here because the Keren-Schul paper gives the vault a sharper methodological critique of explanatory dualism inside reasoning research.
