# Prediction and Models of Judgment

## Scope

This topic will gather the contrast between intuitive judgment and simple formal procedures in prediction and decision support. In the current vault, the key pages are [[Clinical versus actuarial judgment]] and [[Clinical versus actuarial judgment]], now supplemented by the blue-book units [[Kahneman and Tversky 1982 - Intuitive Prediction (in Kahneman et al. 1982, ch. 30)]] and [[Nisbett et al. 1982 - Improving Inductive Inference (in Kahneman et al. 1982, ch. 32)]].
