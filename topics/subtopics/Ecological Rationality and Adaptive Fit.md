# Ecological Rationality and Adaptive Fit

## Scope

This topic will gather the general ecological framework according to which the quality of a decision rule or social order depends on how well it fits the structure of the environment. In the current vault, however, that framework now has to be taught in two versions: the Gigerenzer-style ecology of individual heuristics and the Smith-style ecology of institutions and emergent order. The key pages are therefore [[Ecological rationality]], [[Adaptive toolbox]], [[Todd and Gigerenzer 2007]], [[Constructivist rationality]], [[Smith 2003]], and [[Dekker and Remic 2019]].

## Current focus

The topic page is currently a structural stub so [[Ecological Rationality and Fast-and-Frugal Heuristics]] can function as a readable hub without colliding with the existing concept page [[Ecological rationality]]. A later drafting pass should explain the core framework of adaptive fit more fully for PPE students, but the vault should now already be explicit that [[Dekker and Remic 2019]] distinguishes two ecological-rationality programs rather than one: Gigerenzer's individual-level ecology of heuristics and Smith's institutional ecological rationality.
