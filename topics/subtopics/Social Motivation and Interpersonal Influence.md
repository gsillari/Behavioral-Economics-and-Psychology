# Social Motivation and Interpersonal Influence

## Scope

This topic gathers the parts of the vault where behavior is shaped by fairness, altruism, reciprocity, self-serving interpretation, and interpersonal influence rather than by narrow self-interest alone. In the current vault, the key pages are [[Social preferences]], [[Fairness]], [[Self-serving bias]], and [[Motivated reasoning]], with [[Rabin 1998]] now serving as the broad psychology-and-economics source for this social-motivation branch.

## Current focus

The topic page is currently light so [[Social Behavior, Law, and Public Policy]] can function as a clear hub. A later drafting pass should explain how altruism, reciprocity, inequity aversion, procedural justice, and self-serving fairness judgments fit into the broader architecture of behavioral economics and why they matter for institutions.
