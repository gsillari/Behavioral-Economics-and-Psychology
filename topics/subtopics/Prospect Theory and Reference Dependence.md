A later drafting pass should turn this into a course-facing account of how prospect theory reorganizes the study of risky choice, with [[Tversky and Kahneman 1981 (Framing)]] as one of the best short teaching texts for the gain-loss logic and [[Tversky and Kahneman 1992]] as the mature cumulative version of the theory.
