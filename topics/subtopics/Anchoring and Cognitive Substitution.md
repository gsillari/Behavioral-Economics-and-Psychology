# Anchoring and Cognitive Substitution

## Scope

This topic will gather the mechanisms through which judgments become attached to starting values or simplified proxy questions. In the current vault, the key pages are [[Anchoring]], [[Anchoring]], and [[Attribute substitution]], with [[Strack and Mussweiler 1997]] now giving the anchoring branch a selective-accessibility mechanism alongside the adjustment account.

## Current focus

The topic page is currently a structural stub so [[Judgment, Belief, and Inference]] remains readable as a hub. A later drafting pass should explain the relation between classic anchoring effects, the selective-accessibility account of [[Strack and Mussweiler 1997]], the Epley-Gilovich adjustment framework, and Kahneman’s broader idea that hard questions are often answered through easier substitutions.
