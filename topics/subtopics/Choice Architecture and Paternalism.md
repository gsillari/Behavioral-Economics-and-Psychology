# Choice Architecture and Paternalism

## Scope

This topic will gather the parts of the vault that justify, criticize, and compare behaviorally informed interventions into choice environments. In the current vault, the key pages are [[Libertarian paternalism]], [[Nudge]], [[Asymmetric paternalism]], [[Sunstein 2014]], [[Thaler and Sunstein 2021]], and [[Status quo bias]].

## Current focus

The topic page is currently a structural stub so [[Time, Attention, and Choice Architecture]] can present its second-tier branches clearly. The new first-wave `Nudge` units now give it a stronger backbone: [[Thaler and Sunstein 2021 - Introduction - The Cafeteria]] for the inevitability of choice architecture, [[Thaler and Sunstein 2021 - Chapter 05 - Choice Architecture]] for the design logic itself, and [[Thaler and Sunstein 2021 - Chapter 15 - Much Ado About Nudging]] for the most direct response to criticism. A later drafting pass should still explain the main values and tradeoffs behind behavioral intervention design, including the burden of active choosing, the value of defaults, the force of [[Status quo bias]], and the legitimacy of personalized choice architecture.
