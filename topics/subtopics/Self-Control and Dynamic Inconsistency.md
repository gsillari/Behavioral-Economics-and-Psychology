# Self-Control and Dynamic Inconsistency

## Scope

This topic gathers the parts of the vault concerned with unstable preferences over time and failures of self-control. In the current vault, the key pages are [[Time inconsistency]], [[Projection bias]], and [[Visceral influences on behavior]], with [[O'Donoghue and Rabin 1999]] now serving as the clean formal anchor for present bias, procrastination, preproperation, and sophistication versus naivete.

## Current focus

This topic should explain how intertemporal instability reshapes welfare analysis and policy design. It should build directly on [[O'Donoghue and Rabin 1999]] for the present-bias architecture, [[Loewenstein et al. 2007 - Projection Bias in Predicting Future Utility (in Loewenstein 2007, ch. 13)]] for mistaken forecasts of future tastes, [[Loewenstein and Prelec 2007 - Anomalies in Intertemporal Choice - Evidence and an Interpretation (in Loewenstein 2007, ch. 15)]] for the anomaly-based reconstruction of intertemporal choice, and [[Loewenstein 2007 - Out of Control - Visceral Influences on Behavior (in Loewenstein 2007, ch. 18)]] for hot-state mechanisms.
