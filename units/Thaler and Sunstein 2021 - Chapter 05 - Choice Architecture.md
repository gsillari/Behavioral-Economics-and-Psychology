# Thaler and Sunstein 2021 - Chapter 05 - Choice Architecture

## Summary

This chapter is the conceptual center of the book. It explains what choice architecture is, why design details matter, and how good architecture tries to make actions and signals compatible rather than confusing or error-producing. The chapter gives the nudge program its most reusable language for thinking about defaults, ordering, framing, feedback, and ease.

## Key claims

The first claim is that design is never inert. The architecture of a choice environment affects how people perceive and respond to options.

The second claim is that good design should respect basic psychological regularities rather than work against them.

The third claim is that many apparent mistakes come not from bad motives or bad values, but from poorly designed environments.

The fourth claim is that improving the architecture of decisions can often reduce error without removing options.

## Why it matters for PPE

For PPE students, this chapter matters because it gives the most general design vocabulary in the whole book. It is one of the clearest places where behavioral economics becomes institutional analysis: outcomes depend not only on preferences and incentives, but also on how the environment is built.
