# Thaler and Sunstein 2021 - Preface - Preface to the Final Edition

## Summary

This preface explains why the authors returned to *Nudge* after more than a decade. Its main value in the current vault is architectural rather than conceptual: it marks the distance between the 2008 original and the 2021 final edition, and it frames the revisions as responses to changed technology, changed politics, and years of public criticism.

## Key claims

The first claim is that the original framework still matters because choice architecture remains unavoidable and behaviorally informed policy design remains influential.

The second claim is that the policy environment changed enough to justify revision. The authors explicitly signal new attention to digital choice environments, sludge, durability of interventions, and criticism of nudging.

The third claim is that the final edition should be read not only as a classic statement of the nudge program, but also as a self-conscious update after real-world uptake.

## Why it matters for PPE

For PPE students, this preface matters because it shows how a behavioral-policy framework evolves once it leaves the seminar room and enters institutions. The preface is a reminder that policy ideas should be judged not only by their original elegance, but also by how they age, how they are criticized, and how they adapt.

## Open questions

Does the final edition mainly preserve the original argument, or does it quietly revise it by putting more weight on burden, transparency, and bad choice architecture? How far should later readers treat the 2021 edition rather than the 2008 edition as the canonical text?
