# Maki 2012: Handbook of philosophy of economics

## Summary

[[Maki 2012]] is the top-level work note for *Handbook of Philosophy of Economics*, edited by Uskali Maki. In the current vault, the volume matters as a major philosophy-of-economics reference work rather than as a source of canonical original papers. Its value is architectural: it gathers high-level chapters on realism, models, causation, evidence, explanation, rational choice, game theory, experimentation, behavioral economics, public choice, and related methodological questions in one place.

For this vault, that makes the handbook especially useful as a stabilizing source family. It can strengthen the conceptual and methodological middle layer without displacing the original articles that anchor individual branches.

## Structural map

The volume is divided into two broad parts:

- general philosophical themes
- specific methods, theories, approaches, paradigms, schools, and traditions

For the current vault, the first ingested unit is the Angner and Loewenstein chapter on behavioral economics. That is the right first pass because it gives the handbook an immediate point of contact with an already central branch of the wiki while leaving the rest of the volume mapped for later selective use.

## Main claims

At the work level, the first claim is that economics is methodologically and philosophically heterogeneous in a way that cannot be captured by a single benchmark debate.

The second claim is that many debates that show up in behavioral economics, welfare economics, and policy design are downstream of broader questions about evidence, explanation, realism, rationality, and the ontology of the economic agent.

The third claim is that a philosophy-of-economics handbook can be most useful in this vault when it clarifies existing branches rather than trying to found new ones from scratch.

## Why it matters for PPE

For PPE students, this work matters because it keeps economics connected to philosophy at a level above individual anomalies or policy tools. It matters for economics because it reconstructs the methodological and conceptual background against which specialized literatures operate. It matters for philosophy because it shows how disputes about realism, explanation, and rationality travel into concrete economic theories. And it matters for politics because later policy arguments often inherit these deeper methodological assumptions whether they acknowledge them or not.
