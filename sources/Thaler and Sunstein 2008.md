# Thaler and Sunstein 2008: Nudge

See also: [[Thaler and Sunstein 2021]]

## Summary

[[Thaler and Sunstein 2008]] is the first edition of *Nudge: Improving Decisions About Health, Wealth, and Happiness*. In the current vault, it matters mainly as the original book-length public statement of [[Libertarian paternalism]] and of the claim that choice architecture is unavoidable. It is the historical starting point for the later [[Nudge]] source family, but it is not the edition that should normally anchor current chapter-level ingestion or the most up-to-date representation of the authors' position. That role now belongs to [[Thaler and Sunstein 2021]].

This note is therefore mainly comparative. It records what the 2008 edition originally contained, how the 2021 final edition reorganized and revised it, and why the first edition still matters as a historical baseline for the later policy debate.

## What stays the same across editions

The core architecture of the book survives the transition from 2008 to 2021.

The introduction still carries the cafeteria example and the basic case for nudging.

The first three chapters remain the same in role and in broad argumentative purpose: people are not idealized optimizers, they struggle with temptation, and they are influenced by social information.

Chapter 4 on when nudges are needed and Chapter 5 on choice architecture also remain central in both editions. These are still the book's main conceptual chapters.

At the highest level, both editions defend the same underlying thesis: because choice architecture is inevitable, some forms of behaviorally informed steering can be justified without eliminating options.

## The main structural differences

The 2008 first edition is organized into five parts:

- `Part I: Humans and Econs`
- `Part II: Money`
- `Part III: Health`
- `Part IV: Freedom`
- `Part V: Extensions and Objections`

The 2021 final edition keeps the first part but reorganizes the rest:

- `Part I: Humans and Econs`
- `Part II: The Tools of the Choice Architect`
- `Part III: Money`
- `Part IV: Society`
- `Part V: The Complaints Department`

That change matters. The first edition is more application-driven from the middle onward, while the final edition is more self-conscious about tools, institutional frictions, criticism, and long-run evaluation. The 2021 book is less a collection of policy illustrations and more a retrospective statement of what the nudge program became after years of uptake and criticism.

## Front matter and back matter differences

The 2021 edition begins with a substantial `Preface to the Final Edition`. The 2008 edition does not have that framing layer. The new preface explains why the authors revised the book, which parts were merely updated, which parts were rewritten, and why they added new material on smart disclosure, sludge, climate, long-run effects, and organ donation.

The 2021 edition also adds an `Epilogue`, which the 2008 edition lacks.

The 2008 edition has a separate `Bibliography`. In the 2021 EPUB table of contents, that separate bibliography no longer appears as a standalone back-matter item.

## Chapter-level differences

The cleanest way to compare the two editions is chapter by chapter.

- `Introduction`
  - present in both editions
  - same basic role: introduce the cafeteria example and the idea of nudging

- `Chapters 1-5`
  - present in both editions
  - same basic spine in both books
  - in the final edition these chapters are mostly updated and remastered rather than reconceived

- `2008 Chapter 6: Save More Tomorrow`
  - retained in 2021 as `Chapter 9: Save More Tomorrow`
  - the topic remains central, but the chapter is relocated within a revised structure

- `2008 Chapter 7: Naive Investing`
  - not retained as a standalone chapter in 2021
  - some of the retirement and portfolio-design concerns survive elsewhere, especially in the later discussion of pension design and persistence

- `2008 Chapter 8: Credit Markets`
  - not retained under that title
  - its terrain is partly redistributed into `2021 Chapter 11: Borrow More Today: Mortgages and Credit Cards` and `2021 Chapter 12: Insurance: Don't Sweat the Small Stuff`

- `2008 Chapter 9: Privatizing Social Security: Smorgasbord Style`
  - not retained as a standalone chapter
  - the closest later descendant is `2021 Chapter 10: Do Nudges Last Forever? Perhaps in Sweden`, which returns to pension design but shifts from initial institutional design to the durability of default effects over time

- `2008 Chapter 10: Prescription Drugs: Part D for Daunting`
  - dropped from the final edition as a standalone chapter

- `2008 Chapter 11: How to Increase Organ Donations`
  - retained only in heavily revised form as `2021 Chapter 13: Organ Donations: The Default Solution Illusion`
  - this is one of the most important revisions
  - the final edition rewrites the chapter to make clear that the authors do not endorse presumed consent

- `2008 Chapter 12: Saving the Planet`
  - retained in 2021 as `Chapter 14: Saving the Planet`
  - the topic survives, but the final edition gives more attention to climate change and to the limits of nudges in that domain

- `2008 Chapter 13: Improving School Choices`
  - dropped from the final edition as a standalone chapter

- `2008 Chapter 14: Should Patients Be Forced to Buy Lottery Tickets?`
  - dropped from the final edition as a standalone chapter

- `2008 Chapter 15: Privatizing Marriage`
  - dropped from the final edition
  - this is one of the clearest cases where later political and legal developments overtook the original policy framing

- `2008 Chapter 16: A Dozen Nudges`
  - dropped as a standalone chapter in 2021

- `2008 Chapter 17: Objections`
  - no longer appears under that title
  - much of this terrain is reworked into `2021 Chapter 15: Much Ado About Nudging`

- `2008 Chapter 18: The Real Third Way`
  - no longer appears under that title
  - its justificatory role is also largely absorbed into the later criticism-and-response framing of the final edition

## What the 2021 edition adds

The biggest additions in the final edition are not minor updates. They mark real shifts in emphasis.

`Chapter 6: But Wait, There's More` expands the toolkit around choice architecture rather than moving straight from the core concepts into applications.

`Chapter 7: Smart Disclosure` adds a digital-information and policy-design strand that is simply not present in the same way in 2008.

`Chapter 8: #Sludge` adds a concept that became central to later discussions of administrative burden, friction, and bad choice architecture.

`Chapter 10: Do Nudges Last Forever? Perhaps in Sweden` introduces a retrospective question almost absent from the first edition: how long do nudges actually last?

`Chapter 15: Much Ado About Nudging` gives the final edition a more explicit and concentrated reply to critics than the first edition's objection material.

Overall, the 2021 edition is more reflective, more digitally aware, more sensitive to administrative burden, and more explicit about criticism and limits.

## What the 2008 edition still does better or differently

The first edition gives a clearer picture of what the nudge program looked like before years of uptake, backlash, and revision. It therefore matters as a historical document of the program in its original public form.

It also preserves several first-generation application chapters that the final edition drops entirely. Those chapters may still be useful later if the vault wants the original policy portfolio rather than the later streamlined and revised one.

In that sense, the 2008 edition is not superseded in every respect. It is superseded as the better guide to the authors' mature position, but it remains important as the original statement of the agenda.

## Why this edition matters for the current wiki

In the current vault, this first edition should mainly serve three purposes.

First, it marks the original public form of the `Nudge` project before later revisions.

Second, it gives a stable baseline against which the final edition's additions can be understood. Without the 2008 book in view, the significance of `smart disclosure`, `sludge`, the rewritten organ-donation chapter, and the more explicit reply to critics is harder to see.

Third, it helps keep the policy branch historically honest. The final edition should remain the main edition for active use, but the first edition shows what the original package actually was.
