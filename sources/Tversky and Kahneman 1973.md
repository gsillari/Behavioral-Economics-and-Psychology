# Tversky and Kahneman 1973: Availability
## Summary

[[Tversky and Kahneman 1973]] is the foundational source on [[Availability heuristic]] in the current vault. It explains how judgments of frequency and probability are often guided by the ease with which relevant instances can be recalled or imagined.

This source matters because it gives the availability branch a direct primary anchor rather than leaving it to be reconstructed mainly from the shorter overview treatment in [[Tversky and Kahneman 1974]]. It also provides the classic mechanisms behind recall bias, imaginability effects, and illusory correlation.

## Key claims

The first claim is that ease of recall and ease of construction influence frequency and probability judgments.

The second claim is that these cues often track real-world frequency imperfectly and therefore create systematic bias.

The third claim is that availability is a distinct heuristic family, not just a special case of representativeness or anchoring.

## Why this source matters in the vault

This source should now anchor [[Availability heuristic]] directly and give the availability branch the same historical depth that the representativeness branch already has. It also helps clarify how later risk-perception sources build on a more basic cognitive mechanism.

In the current vault, [[Tversky and Kahneman 1974]] remains the canonical broad statement of the program. [[Tversky and Kahneman 1973]] gives one of its major mechanisms a more complete original foundation.

## Why it matters for PPE

For PPE students, this paper matters because it shows how an apparently simple shortcut can distort beliefs in ways that later matter for markets, politics, and law. It matters for economics because perceived frequency and probability shape expectation, demand, and precaution. It matters for philosophy because it sharpens the contrast between intuitive and extensional reasoning. It matters for politics because salience-sensitive judgment helps explain why some public problems dominate attention while others are neglected.
